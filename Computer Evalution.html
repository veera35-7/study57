Machines Learn to Think

Artificial Intelligence — inspired by the human brain

Scientists built artificial neural networks — loosely inspired by brain cells. Instead of being told every rule, machines started learning from examples. This is Artificial Intelligence.

Now Machines Can See

Computer Vision — the eye of Artificial Intelligence

👁️ What the camera sees
🧠 What the AI detects
Computer Vision lets machines understand images — reading faces, spotting tumours, driving cars, recognising handwriting. In milliseconds, they see what took nature millions of years to evolve.
